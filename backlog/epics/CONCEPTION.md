A Conception is the PhaseRun of a retained Idée through the « pour chaque Idée retenue » Phase of its Projet's Process (ADR 0011), started from the Idée (US-8) and followed Étape by Étape as that Process gives them (US-10): in « Process Crewdeck » the PRD, the mockups, the technical design, the cross review and the Découpage, each an official Skill checked against its Contrat; each document reviewed and approved (a merge bound to its SHA on the code's Source, GitHub or Local, an Action sensible) or sent back with changes (US-11); the Tickets validated in one pass and written to the Tickets' Source through the Miroir (US-12).

Milestone: M6 Conception. Stories US-8, US-10, US-11, US-12, US-26.
