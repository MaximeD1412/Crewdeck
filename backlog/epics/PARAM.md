The Paramètres (US-82): the account's, as the defaults, and each Projet's, inheriting them and changing only what it needs; the Moteur and model of each Rôle (US-39), the Review on another Moteur than the Code by default; the Rôles the Pilote adds (a review by Gemini); a Projet's integration and release branches and its Commandes « Préparer le worktree » and « Nettoyer le worktree » (ADR 0011). The Étapes optionnelles are gone (US-83 removed): an Étape runs because it is in the Projet's Process.

Milestones: M1 Socle (PARAM-1), M2 Runner and Projets (PARAM-2), M3 Sessions and preparing Projets (PARAM-3). Stories US-39, US-82.
