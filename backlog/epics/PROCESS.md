The Process of each Projet as a versioned `ProcessDefinition` (ADR 0011): its Phases, Étapes, typed Entrées and Sorties, arrows, Retours and Outils; the catalogue of Étapes and the Étapes du produit; the pure rules shared by the page and the server (`validateDefinition`, `findGaps`, `diff`); the draft saved on every change and activated as an Action sensible; the « Process Crewdeck » Modèle and the Pilote's own Modèles; the canvas at 1280 and its read-only list at 390; and the engine (`PhaseRun`, `StepRun`, the kind handlers, « pour chaque » admission, lineage, Retours with their maximum, the Commande Étape, the « Livraison » Phase).

Milestones: M3 Sessions and preparing Projets (the definition, the rules, the draft and the engine), M4 Boucle (the canvas, activation, Commandes, Livraison, Modèles). Stories US-92 to US-98, US-104, US-105, US-111. Risks AGT-9, SEC-11.
