The Composants of ADR 0006 and the default Skin « Passerelle » (`design/DESIGN.md`): `defineComposant` and `defineSkin` in `@crewdeck/sdk`, the registry, the `cd-action` bridge, the router and `t()`; the library page `/parametres/composants` generated from the declarations (US-23); then every Composant of the mockups' catalogue, by family, each with its `examples`, previewed on that page; and the Cadre (AppShell). Pages are built by the domain tickets from these Composants.

Then the Composants of « Gestion du process » (ProcessCanvas, Phase, StepCard, Port, ToolChip, LoopBack, Toast, PhaseGroup, StepRow, Stepper, Menu, the compact Cadre).

Milestones: M1 Socle, M4 Boucle (UI-8, UI-9). Stories US-23, US-92, US-93, US-98. Risk AGT-4, SEC-5.

Order: UI-1 → UI-2 → UI-3, UI-4, UI-5, UI-6, UI-7; UI-8, UI-9.
