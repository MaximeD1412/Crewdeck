Actualités — PRD

Feature of Site mairie. Idea: « Publier les actualités de la mairie sur la page d’accueil ».

Problem

  • The town hall posts its news on a notice board and on social media; the website shows none of it.
  • Residents miss the council’s decisions and the road works.

Stories

  • US-3 (MVP) As an editor at the town hall, I want to publish a news item with a title, a date and a picture, so that residents read it on the website.
  • US-4 (MVP) As a resident, I want the three latest news items on the home page, so that I see what is new at a glance.
  • US-5 (MVP) As a resident, I want news older than one year archived, the council’s orders staying online whatever their age, so that the list stays short.
  • US-6 (next) As an editor, I want to schedule a news item, so that it goes out on the day of the event.

Open questions

  • Who at the town hall may publish without a second reading?
