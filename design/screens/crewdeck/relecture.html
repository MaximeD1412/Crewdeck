Agenda partagé — PRD

Feature of Personal OS. Idea: « Partager mon agenda avec ma famille ».

Problem

  • Each member keeps their own agenda; shared events are copied by hand and drift apart.
  • Nobody knows who changed an event, nor when.

Stories

  • US-14 (MVP) As a member, I want to see the events of a shared agenda in my agenda, so that I plan around them.
  • US-15 (MVP) As the owner, I want to invite a member by email, so that they join the agenda.
  • US-16 (MVP) As a member, I want to edit a shared event, every member being allowed to edit it, so that the agenda stays up to date.
  • US-17 (MVP) As a member, I want recurring shared events, so that weekly plans are entered once.
  • US-18 (next) As a member, I want to leave a shared agenda, so that its events leave my agenda.

Open questions

  • What does a member who leaves keep of the events they created?
  • Is an invitation by link allowed, or only by email?
